# Safety and use disclaimer

This is research-prototype hardware documentation. It is not clinically
validated, certified as a medical device, electrically qualified or approved
for unattended use.

Do not copy pin assignments into hardware blindly. Verify the exact controller
revision, voltage levels, current limits, reset states, polarity, protection,
load behavior and travel limits. Begin with outputs and motor drivers disabled,
use current-limited test equipment and maintain a physical power-removal path.
