# Contributing

Start with `STATUS.md`, `LICENSING.md`, and the hardware safety guardrails.
Clearly separate observed prototype wiring from candidates and future designs.
Every pin, voltage, current, board revision, and connector claim needs a source
or bounded measurement record.

Do not present an unverified schematic or pin assignment as safe to build. Do
not attach patient/specimen identity, private deployment details, or
credentials. Source contributions are accepted under the license boundary
documented in `LICENSING.md`.

By contributing original material, the contributor agrees to license it under
the license assigned to its path in `REUSE.toml` and confirms that they have
authority to do so.
