# Authors

The prototype uses a custom Cartesian linear stage that predates the controller
and imaging integration documented here. Its contributor remains anonymous;
this repository records the interface facts required to operate the prototype
without claiming authorship of the underlying mechanical design.

**Alexander Fridman** developed the MKS controller integration,
motor-control configuration, Arduino/MKS illumination-control architecture,
RG-focus optical integration and associated hardware documentation in this
snapshot.

References to third-party boards, cameras and open-source projects identify
their respective products and sources; they do not imply endorsement.
